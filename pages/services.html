01
Cultural Heritage

Historical Photo Gallery

At the heart of the Limburg Center is a dedicated exhibition hall displaying photographs, artifacts, and paintings from the German Colonial era in Cameroon. Every exhibit carries a contextual caption — educating visitors while honoring the shared history between our two nations.

Whether you are a history enthusiast, a researcher, a tourist, or a student — the gallery offers a unique window into a defining era of Cameroon's past.

02
A1 to B2 Certification

German Language School

Our German Language School offers internationally recognized A1–B2 certification with monthly intakes. Choose from Morning, Evening, Weekend, or Online classes. Each cohort runs for 2 months, 5 days per week, 2h30 per day.

  • A1, A2, B1, B2 levels — Visa, Ausbildung & University preparation
  • Small group sizes for personal attention
  • Physical classes in Limbe + remote Online option
  • Monthly intake — start when you are ready
  • Student portal with live Jitsi classroom access

03
Knowledge Hub

German Library

Our library is a quiet research and reading environment stocked with German books on history, science, technology, fiction, and professional development. Members also enjoy access to a growing e-book collection and German movies.

The library supports students from our German Language Clubs in local schools and universities, providing a dedicated space for focused study.

  • Physical books: history, science, fiction, academic, professional
  • E-book and digital resource access for members
  • German movies and documentaries — permanently running
  • Quiet study tables and comfortable seating
  • Free Wi-Fi for research and study

04
Community & Networking

Events & Conferences

Every month, Limburg Cultural Center organises at least two panel discussions on trending research topics about Germany. We also host sociocultural, academic, professional, and business events to strengthen German-Cameroon cooperation ties.

Our event spaces are also available for hire by organizations and institutions seeking a professional cultural venue in Limbe.

  • Monthly panel discussions — open to the public
  • Sociocultural and academic conferences
  • Business networking and trade events
  • Cultural evenings: film screenings, art exhibitions, music
  • Venue hire available for external organizations

05
Skills & Employment

Vocational Training

To enable youths to take advantage of the growing demand for skilled workers in Cameroon and Germany, our Vocational Training Department offers practical, career-ready courses across multiple fields.

Graduates are better equipped to enter the job market in Cameroon and to pursue opportunities in Germany through the Ausbildung (apprenticeship) system.

Fashion Design Esthetics Hair Dressing Tourism Cabin Crew Driving Information Technology Handicrafts Dancing Cake Artistry Engineering Hotel & Catering Office Administration Accounting Management & Finance

06
Haus der Freundschaft

Café & Restaurant

The Haus der Freundschaft (House of Friendship) is a relaxation terrace, snack bar, and restaurant with an emphasis on German cuisine and drinks. It is a daily meeting point for German workers in Cameroon, the diaspora, and fans of German culture in Limbe.

Bundesliga matches are shown live on LED screens. Businessmen schedule meetings here. Students unwind after class. Everyone is welcome.

  • German cuisine and liqueurs — authentic flavors
  • Bundesliga live on LED screens every matchday
  • Relaxation terrace — ideal for meetings and social gatherings
  • Snack bar and full restaurant service
  • Private event bookings and catering available

07
Explore Cameroon

Tourism Office

Our Tourism Office offers unique package tours to the most spectacular sites in Cameroon — designed for both Cameroonian explorers and international visitors from Germany and beyond. We target 100 German tourists per month.

  • Limbe Botanic Garden & Wildlife Centre
  • Mount Cameroon guided treks and volcano experiences
  • German colonial lighthouse and heritage site tours
  • Mangrove trails, beaches, caves, and waterfalls
  • Partner hotels at preferential rates for all budgets
  • Travel agency and international ticketing services

08
Getting Around

Transport Services

The Limburg Center operates a bus transport service for tourists and members. We are also committed to social inclusion — providing affordable transport to underprivileged students in Limbe who cannot afford daily school transport.

  • Tour transport — from the center to all tour sites
  • Member shuttles for events and activities
  • Subsidized school transport for disadvantaged pupils
  • Airport and hotel transfers for visiting German tourists
